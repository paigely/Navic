<p>Navic is a free and open source modern Navidrome client for Android and iOS.</p>

<h2>Features</h2>
<ul>
	<li>Customisable: large selection of settings and tweaks</li>
	<li>Secure & private: minimal permissions, zero telemetry, and zero analytics</li>
	<li>Integrated: shows up on the lock screen + quick settings</li>
	<li>Lightweight & fast: zero bloat</li>
	<li>Feature rich: covers almost the entirety of the Subsonic API</li>
	<li>Works offline: syncs your entire library locally, and allows you to download songs</li>
</ul>
